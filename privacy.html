Privacy Policy Last updated: 31 July 2026 Subliminal Messaging ("we", "us", "our") operates the Subliminal mobile application (the "App"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have. By using Subliminal, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. Who We Are Subliminal Messaging is based in Melbourne, Australia. For any question about this policy or your data, contact us at hello@sub-liminalapp.com. Information We Collect Account information When you create an account, we collect your email address and a password (stored securely, we never see your plain password). Voice recordings If you choose to use voice cloning, we collect a short recording of your voice. This recording is sent to our voice generation partner, ElevenLabs, to create a cloned voice used only for generating your personal audio content. Content you create This includes your manifestations, Sunday reflections, and any written or spoken input you provide, such as answers given during onboarding. This content is used to generate personalized audio stories and affirmation tracks for you. Usage information We collect basic information about how you use the App, such as which tracks you play, how often you open the App, and general device information (device type, operating system version), to help us fix problems and improve the App. Purchase information Subscription purchases are handled by Apple's App Store and processed through RevenueCat, our subscription management partner. We do not see or store your payment card details. How We Use Your Information We use your information to: Create your account and let you sign in Generate personalized audio stories and affirmation tracks based on what you share with us, using AI services including Anthropic (for story text) and ElevenLabs (for voice generation) Maintain and improve the App Provide support if you contact us Manage your subscription We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not use your data for advertising. Who We Share Information With We share limited information with the following service providers, only as needed to run the App: Firebase (Google): authentication, data storage, and file storage Anthropic: to generate the text of your personalized future self stories ElevenLabs: to generate voice audio, including cloned voice audio if you opt in RevenueCat: to manage and verify your subscription Apple: to process App Store purchases These providers only receive the information needed to perform their specific function and are not permitted to use your information for their own purposes. We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users. Data Storage and Security Your data is stored using Firebase's secure cloud infrastructure. We apply reasonable technical measures to protect your information, though no method of storage or transmission is ever completely secure. Voice recordings used for cloning are processed by ElevenLabs under their own data handling terms. You can find more about how ElevenLabs handles data on their website. Data Retention We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to keep certain records by law. Your Rights Depending on where you live, you may have the right to: Access the personal information we hold about you Ask us to correct inaccurate information Ask us to delete your account and associated data Export a copy of your data To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@sub-liminalapp.com. Children's Privacy Subliminal is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it. International Data Transfers Because our service providers operate internationally, your information may be processed in countries outside Australia, including the United States. We take steps to ensure your information continues to receive an appropriate level of protection wherever it is processed. Changes to This Policy We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will let you know within the App or by email before the changes take effect.
